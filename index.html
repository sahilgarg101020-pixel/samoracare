FAQ
Got questions? Get answers.
What is SSDI?

Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) is a federal program that provides monthly income to people who can no longer work due to a medical condition expected to last at least 12 months. If you've worked and paid Social Security taxes, you may be eligible.

How much does it cost?

Our eligibility screening is completely free. If we connect you with a lawyer, they work on a contingency basis — you pay nothing unless your claim is approved. Attorney fees are capped by law at 25% of back pay, with a maximum of $7,200.

What happens after I complete the screening?

We'll review your responses and reach out within 24 hours to discuss your case. If you qualify, we'll connect you with a vetted SSDI attorney in your area who will guide you through the application or appeal process.

What if I've already been denied?

Don't give up. Over 50% of SSDI claims are initially denied, but many are overturned on appeal — especially with legal representation. An experienced attorney can help strengthen your case and represent you at hearings.

How long does the SSDI process take?

Initial applications typically take 3-6 months. If an appeal is needed, it can take 12-18 months. Having an attorney from the start often speeds things up and increases your chances of approval.
